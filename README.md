<summary><sub>How it's made</sub></summary>
<br>

- The third piece of Horizontes (Espacio · Tiempo · Destino · Infinito): one scroll, about ten minutes, from «is anyone out there?» to the last possible work, with a civilization's power on a Kardashev ladder.
- Three.js and TypeScript, no framework. One shared world in astronomical units with a pivoting camera, so the metres of a space elevator and the hundred thousand light years of the galaxy are exact in the same frame.
- Everything is points: the planets and the Sun come from a shared library of bodies drawn in points over real maps (Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0).
- Keys: A plays, S sound, I the plate (labels, diagrams and the sources of every figure), L language. English and Spanish.
- `npm install`, `npm run dev`. The clip above is recorded from the piece itself: `node tools/clip.mjs`, then `python tools/readme.py`.

</details>
